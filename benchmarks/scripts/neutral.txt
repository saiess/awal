Every time you open a tap, you take part in a journey that began far from your kitchen. Rain falls on hills, collects in rivers, and slowly enters underground layers of rock. Along the way, water carries tiny particles and dissolved minerals. Treatment plants remove unwanted material, check the water, and send it through a network of pipes. Pressure keeps that water moving, even when the road rises uphill. A storage tank helps the system meet the rush of demand each morning. The next time you fill a glass, pause for a moment. That ordinary action depends on careful engineering, regular testing, and thousands of people doing work that usually stays out of sight.
