At dawn, the island looked deserted. Then a light appeared in the old lighthouse. "Did you see that?" Maya whispered. Her brother stopped rowing. For thirty years, nobody had lived beyond the harbor wall, yet someone was climbing the spiral stairs. A door slammed. Three gulls lifted into the wind, and the light went dark. Was it a warning, a mistake, or simply a reflection from the rising sun? The answer would begin with a notebook, a rusted key, and a photograph taken before either of them was born. By sunset, one small discovery would change the story everyone in the village thought they knew. But first, they had to reach the shore.
